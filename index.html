About Me

Building Systems That
See Through the Noise

I'm a data engineering and machine learning developer currently pursuing my Master of Computer Applications at RNS Institute of Technology, Bengaluru. My work sits at the intersection of raw, messy data and intelligent systems that act on it.

I spent time building end-to-end data pipelines — ingestion, cleaning, feature extraction, quality assurance — turning unstructured URL datasets into structured, analysis-ready formats. My flagship project, a Phishing URL Detection System, is a full-stack ML pipeline from raw input to model prediction.

I care deeply about pipeline reproducibility, data consistency, and writing Python logic that scales. When I'm not writing code, I'm studying system design and pushing my problem-solving on HackerRank.
